## 1. booking_centre (highest priority)

### 1f. NEW, confirmed bug: replacement offers computed on a stale agenda clock → −1
- Journey `099` (priority D, `S` = 520 slots): offer at 22:24, the citizen confirms 16 real minutes later (22:40) = 16 agenda hours. The facility cancels the slot, which is now past; booking_centre replies within 2 seconds with **slots already in the past** (`h2` 11-12 10:30 and 12:00), the citizen refuses with `reason=past`, no booking while `S` was non-empty → `wrong_outcome` −1.
- Cause in `booking_centre/agent/agent.py` (origin/master `5f949d5`): on a facility `cancel`, `_advance_alone` sets `refresh=True` and asks the Ledger for a fresh `agenda`, but `fresh()` reads `_after(chat, sent.mark)`. When the mark is not yet in `chat` (the ask has only just left), `_after` **returns the whole case** ("No mark, or a mark history does not show: the whole case"), so `_has_agenda` finds the **old** agenda from 16 minutes earlier and `_latest_slots` the old world. `fresh` is already true, the wait doesn't happen, and `now` = stale `agenda_now`. The same happens if `_say` returns `"sent"` (fallback when `city.say` returns no id): that mark never shows up in history.
- To change: after a fresh ask, consider only Ledger answers **after** the mark (if the mark isn't in history yet, wait for it rather than falling back to the whole case); as an extra guard, correct `now` with the real time elapsed since the Ledger answer (×60) before `fitting`, so a slot already past can never be offered.

### 1g. NEW: booking_centre keeps offering after the facility has booked
- Journey `112`: the hospital sends `booking.receipt` (commit ok, `booking_in_pref` +2), but in the same second booking_centre tells the citizen "the facility has cancelled this slot" and offers more; each `confirm` ends in another cancel, 6 rounds and 7 holds. The score holds (+2) but the journey ends `failed` and leaves holds behind.
- To change: in `_partner_cancel`, if `_booking_stage(...)` is already `done` (a receipt has reached the citizen), don't tell the citizen about a cancel and don't call `_advance`. The cancel's reason isn't in the console (agent↔agent messages aren't shown): read it in `city.history` for need `112`.

## 2. gp

- **Technical tokens up front.** Every prescription reply starts with `prescription prescription_number=… procedure=… priority=…` before the human sentence (see `gp/agent/agent.py` around line 132 on origin/master). Escalations are just `escalate.emergency reason=red_flag Contatti subito il 112.`, with no empathy sentence in the older replies. The judge accepts the protocol (always 2), but sentiment on gp replies averages −0.17 and citizens read it as curt. Put the human sentence first and keep the tokens at the end. Check with `mach-hackathon-kit/docs/kit/e19-rules.md` that the token position doesn't matter to the Ledger before moving them.
- **Language.** In some journeys (`054`, `071`, `106`) gp replied in Italian to English messages; others (`094`, `100`) are in English, and `106` (22:31 UTC) is still Italian, so it isn't fixed everywhere. Make gp always mirror the citizen's language.
- **Proactivity 0 in 95 journeys.** The review gives a point when the agent acts unprompted. The obvious lever is gp handing off to booking_centre itself after the prescription, which is already partly done ("il suo medico di base mi ha inoltrato la ricetta"). Read the review `notes` of a gp journey that got proactivity 1 to see what earned it.
